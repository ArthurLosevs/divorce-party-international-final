# G. Board recommendation

**Do not value DPI or calculate an earn-out from the management accounts.** The €312k management profit is not sufficiently reliable for either purpose. My current base-case profit is **€65k**, meaning the management figure is **€247k too high and approximately 4.8× the reconstructed profit**.

I would allow the **core business to continue under a controlled turnaround**, rather than immediately conclude it is uneconomic. The reconstructed business still produces €77k operating profit and €65k after interest. The acute problem is not simply operating profitability; it is weak controls, owner extraction, poor working-capital discipline and accounting manipulation.

Liquidity needs immediate attention. Closing cash is only **€60k**, versus €126k supplier payables, €32k payroll payable and €2k interest payable even before the legal provision and future customer obligations. The loan's maturity is unknown. Also, reported CFO of €139k includes **€90k of cash received for September work and €35k collection of opening AR**, so the headline operating cash figure should not be treated as fully recurring cash generation.

Before valuation I recommend:

1. **Freeze owner-card and related-party spending immediately.**
2. Obtain debtor/platform confirmations for the remaining €168k net AR.
3. Perform a SKU-level inventory reconciliation to resolve the €9k difference.
4. Obtain the actual fixed-asset/depreciation schedule and insurance documentation.
5. Obtain full loan maturity/covenant terms and build a 13-week liquidity forecast.

The corrected accounts should then be formally certified before any purchase-price adjustment or earn-out is fixed.
