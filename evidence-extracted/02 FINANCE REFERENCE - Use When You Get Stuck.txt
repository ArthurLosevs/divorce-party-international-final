Finance Reference for the Hostile Takeover Case  
Open 01 START HERE first  Use this document when a finance concept blocks you 
This is not a second assignment and it is not the first file to read. Inspect the case evidence first. Return here 
when you need to understand how a transaction affects profit, cash, assets, liabilities or equity. 
1 The accounting equation 
Assets equal liabilities plus equity. Every correction must preserve this equation. A balanced statement can still 
contain wrong classifications, but an unbalanced statement is definitely incomplete or wrong. 
2 Profit is not cash 
Revenue and expense follow economic activity. Cash flow follows money. A customer deposit can increase cash 
without increasing revenue. A loan increases cash and debt, not profit. 
3 Revenue and receivables 
Recognize revenue when the promised goods or service are delivered. Closing receivables equal opening 
receivables plus credit revenue minus collections and write-offs. Deposits for future work are liabilities. 
4 Inventory and cost of goods sold 
Closing inventory equals opening inventory plus purchases minus COGS and write-offs. Physical existence does 
not guarantee financial value. Unsaleable damaged goods require a write-down. 
5 Payroll and accruals 
Payroll expense belongs to the period in which employees worked. Cash paid may differ. Closing payroll liability 
equals opening liability plus expense minus cash paid. 
6 Fixed assets repairs and depreciation 
Capitalize spending that creates or improves a long-term resource. Expense work that merely restores normal 
operation. Depreciation allocates asset cost over useful life and is non-cash in the current period. 
7 Loans interest and owner money 
Borrowing and principal repayment are financing activities. Interest is an expense. Personal owner spending is 
a distribution unless genuine business purpose and evidence support another treatment.

8 Provisions and later evidence 
Recognize a provision when an obligation from a past event is probable and can be estimated. Evidence received 
after period end can confirm a condition that already existed at the reporting date. 
9 Reconstructing the three statements 
Build the supporting schedules first. Use them to produce the P&L, then connect cash flow and the balance 
sheet. Link values rather than typing the same number repeatedly. Reconcile cash, working capital, debt, PPE 
and equity. 
10 Evidence discipline 
Prefer bank and signed third-party evidence over unsupported management claims. Preserve competing 
interpretations, quantify their effect, state confidence and make the final judgment yourself. 
Minimum board output 
• Corrected profit and cash position. 
• Working-capital and solvency warning. 
• Five immediate control actions. 
• Key uncertainty ranges. 
• A decision on whether the core business should continue.
