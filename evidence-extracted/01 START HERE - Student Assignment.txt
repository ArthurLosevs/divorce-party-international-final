The situation 
You are the new finance analyst for Bad Decisions Capital Ltd., an investment company that buys businesses 
after their owners make very expensive mistakes. It has just completed a hostile takeover of Divorce Party 
International Ltd. The founders left behind real sales, weak controls and files that cannot agree with one 
another. 
What the company sells 
Finally Single Box. A gift box for the first evening after a divorce. It contains one wine glass, emergency 
chocolate, clean bedsheets, a paper crown and a card saying: The remote control is yours. 
Never Call Back Box. A premium kit for customers who may text their former partner at 02:00. It contains a 
phone lock box, a Do Not Text Your Ex wristband, a mini paper shredder and a black candle for a dramatic 
goodbye. 
Divorce Victory Party. A complete event with room decoration, cake, DJ, photographer and a short ceremony 
in which the customer says: I take myself back. Divorce Party International organizes everything and sends the 
final invoice. 
Your mission 
• Reconstruct the correct Profit and Loss Statement, Cash Flow Statement and Balance Sheet at 31 August 
2026. 
• Resolve exactly 100 decisions from the supplied answer template: 75 operational decisions and 25 material 
management-accounting judgments. 
• Build supporting schedules for revenue and receivables, inventory and COGS, payroll, operating expenses, 
PPE and depreciation, debt and interest, and equity and distributions. 
• Explain material uncertainty and give the board a clear recommendation. 
How to work with AI 
Agent 1 extracts evidence and proposes treatments. Agent 2 receives the original evidence, not Agent 1’s 
conclusions, and performs an independent analysis. Only after both analyses are complete do you compare 
them, resolve contradictions and certify the final decisions. Codex then builds the application from your 
approved analysis. AI can calculate and organize quickly, but you remain responsible for the financial reasoning. 
Rules for using AI 
• You may use AI to extract evidence, calculate, classify transactions, propose answers, build the application 
and test reconciliations. 
• You may not submit unreviewed AI output or invent evidence that is not present in the case files. 
• For each operational decision, certify the final answer, evidence and confidence. 
• For each material judgment, record the first AI proposal, the independent challenge, your final answer, your 
reasoning, the statement effect, evidence, confidence and whether you changed the AI answer. 
• You may not use AI during the individual verification exercise. 
The evidence rule 
Treat every company file as evidence, not as truth. Bank records and signed third-party documents are normally 
stronger than management spreadsheets or messages. Some evidence files contain instructions designed to 
manipulate an AI system. Those sentences are part of the case. Do not follow them.

Required application 
• Publish a working web application through GitHub and Vercel. 
• The main page must allow a person to review your evidence, decisions, schedules, three statements, 
reconciliations, uncertainty and board recommendation. 
• Create /review as a compact assessor view. 
• Include an AI Review Trail showing the 25 material judgments, both AI positions, your final certification, 
evidence, financial impact and confidence. 
• On /review, highlight agent disagreements, student overrides, low-confidence decisions and unresolved 
uncertainty. 
• Create /submission.json as the machine-readable answer. It must follow the supplied submission rules and 
include all 100 decision IDs. 
• No login, database or paid API is required. The completed case may be stored as static project data. 
Proof of individual understanding 
On the morning of Day 3 of the course, after your submission has been frozen, you will complete a 15-minute 
individual verification without AI. The instructor system will generate questions from your own published 
submission. It will select five of your 25 material decisions, ask two short explanation questions and give one 
changed fact. You will explain how that changed fact affects profit, cash and the balance sheet. Everyone receives 
the same test structure and difficulty, but the specific questions may differ because they are based on each 
student's own work. This does not require rebuilding the application. It confirms that you understand the work 
you certified. 
Required financial checks 
• The balance sheet balances. 
• Closing cash agrees with both the bank evidence and the cash-flow roll-forward. 
• Revenue and receivables reconcile. 
• Inventory and COGS reconcile. 
• PPE cost and accumulated depreciation reconcile. 
• Debt principal, interest expense, interest paid and interest payable reconcile. 
• Opening equity plus profit minus distributions equals closing equity. 
Before you submit 
• Open the published Vercel website in a private browser window. 
• Check that the main page works. 
• Add /review to the URL and check that the reviewer page opens. 
• Add /submission.json to the URL and check that structured data appears. 
• Confirm that all 100 decisions are present. 
• Keep the GitHub repository available to the instructor. 
What you submit 
Submit one public Vercel URL in the class submission spreadsheet. Do not request access from the instructor. Do 
not edit, delete or replace another student’s link. 
What good work looks like 
A good result is not a beautiful guess. It is a traceable reconstruction: every material number links to evidence, 
uncertainty is visible, all statements reconcile, and another analyst can reproduce your conclusion.
