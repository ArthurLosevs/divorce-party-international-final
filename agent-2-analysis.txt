# G. Board recommendation

The company is **profitable on the reconstructed baseline, but nowhere near management's claimed level**. Corrected profit of **€65k** is only about **6.8% of revenue**, versus the €312k presentation used by management. Closing cash is only €60k while confirmed and accrued obligations are substantial.

I would recommend the board **continue the underlying operating business conditionally**, because the delivered sales themselves appear real and supported by customer/bank evidence, but **freeze valuation and any earn-out calculation based on the €312k management profit**.

Before a final acquisition valuation or earn-out certification, I would require: a SKU-level inventory reconciliation resolving the €9k conflict; an asset-level depreciation schedule with useful lives; complete founder-card receipts and formal treatment of the €110k distributions; confirmation of opening AR/AP/equity; and the signed earn-out definition. Controls should immediately prohibit owner personal spending, require revenue cut-off tied to delivery/acceptance, reconcile bank/AR/AP monthly, require formal physical inventory counts, and force month-end accrual/provision/depreciation entries.
